<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02985">Temporal Leakage in LLM Backtesting: Measurement, Validation, and Adjusted Scores</a> is officially accepted at <b>TMLR</b>, with a decision of <a href="https://openreview.net/forum?id=x3qagPELrp">"accept as is."</a> That completes the backtesting trilogy alongside NeurIPS 2026 and the Findings of EMNLP 2026. This paper supplies the theory: leakage inflation cannot be identified from a passive backtest alone, and one external reference restores a leakage-adjusted score. <a href="https://github.com/ZeyuZhang1901/Temporal-Leakage-Backtesting">Code and data</a> are public.
