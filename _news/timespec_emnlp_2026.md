<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17234">All Leaks Count, Some Count More</a> is accepted to the <b>Findings of EMNLP 2026</b> (14.3% acceptance)! The paper measures how much of an LLM's decision-driving reasoning is temporally contaminated (Shapley-DCLR) and blocks it at inference time without retraining (TimeSPEC).
