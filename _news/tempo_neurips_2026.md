<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18843">TEMPO</a> is accepted to <b>NeurIPS 2026</b> (main track, 25.7% acceptance)! Rather than erasing what an LLM knows, TEMPO teaches it temporal discipline: a two-mode RL reward first drives post-cutoff leakage to zero, then optimizes prediction quality, with guarantees that leakage decreases monotonically along the way.
