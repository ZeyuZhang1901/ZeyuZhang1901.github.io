Zion & Grand Canyon

March 2026

A March loop through Utah and Arizona: red rock, cold rivers, and a lot of staring. In Zion we waded the river until our legs went numb and laughed the whole way. In Page, Lower Antelope Canyon turned out to be a sculpture that light re-carves every hour, and Horseshoe Bend showed us the river's slowest, grandest U-turn. Then the Grand Canyon itself — no photo prepares you for the moment the ground simply stops. Wild, enormous, and completely indifferent to us. I loved it.
