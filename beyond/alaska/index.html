Alaska

March 2025

Alaska in March is a world with the color turned down and the volume turned up. We rode the old Alaska Railroad north, watched sea lions bicker over a sunny rock near Seward, and stepped inside a glacier hole so blue it didn't look real. In North Pole the reindeer never stopped eating; on the Dalton Highway we drove until the map said Arctic Circle. And when the aurora finally spilled green over Fairbanks, nobody remembered the cold. Denali saw us off with a proper blizzard — and the softest snow I've ever fallen into.
