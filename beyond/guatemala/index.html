Guatemala

May 2026

Guatemala doesn't do quiet. Tuk-tuks buzz around Lake Atitlán, markets spill into the streets, and a wedding can simply take over a corner in Antigua — everyone approves. We drifted between lakeside towns, found hippie yoga in San Marcos and an artist's studio I left three paintings richer, then climbed Acatenango for seven relentless hours. That night Fuego erupted over and over, like the mountain had something to say. At sunrise, above the clouds, it stopped feeling like a view and started feeling like a blessing.
