Back Home — China

Summer 2026

Some trips you plan; this one you simply return to. A few weeks back home in China with my family, and the itinerary wrote itself: slow mornings in the neighborhood garden — there's a little farm in it, the air is fresh, and everything smells like things growing — lazy afternoons around Meibei Lake, all willows, bridges, and pagodas, and a full-time eating schedule. Liangpi, roujiamo, yangrou paomo, plus a Cantonese detour of chicken pot and dim sum. No summits, no border stamps. Just home — which, it turns out, is a destination too.
